# Previous mistakes

Previous mistakes are a record of the flashcards that you marked as "Don't know" while studying with progressive mode. They can be reviewed at any time to target the cards that give you the most trouble, and practiced by turning them into a new study set.

## Recording mistakes

Mistakes are recorded automatically while studying flashcards. Whenever a card is marked as "Don't know" in progressive mode, it is stored as a previous mistake along with the study set it belongs to. Traditional mode does not record mistakes.

Each card is only recorded once per studying session, even if it is marked as "Don't know" in more than one round.

:::info
See [Flashcards](/usage/flashcards) for how to study a set using progressive mode.
:::

## Viewing previous mistakes

To view your previous mistakes, click "Previous mistakes" in the navigation bar.

Mistakes are grouped by study set, with the study set containing your most recent mistake shown first. Within each group, mistakes are sorted from the most recent to the oldest, and each card shows the date and time it was recorded.

If you haven't made any mistakes yet, the page will state this and encourage you to keep studying.

## Practicing your mistakes

When mistakes are present, they can be turned into a new study set to practice the cards you did not know.

Click on the mistake cards under "Create a study set from your mistakes" to select the ones you want to practice, give your study set a title, then click "Create study set". You will be redirected to the new set once it is created, where you can study it with flashcards or quizzes like any other set.

:::info
The new study set is created with private visibility, meaning it is only viewable by you. This can be changed at any time by editing the set.
:::

## Removing mistakes

Individual mistakes can be removed by clicking the trash icon in the top right corner of the mistake card. This only removes the record of the mistake - the card itself remains part of its study set.

Mistakes are also removed automatically if the card or study set that they belong to is deleted.
